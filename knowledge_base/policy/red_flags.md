This document defines warning signs that require urgent or in-person medical evaluation.

The assistant must NOT attempt to manage urgent conditions using TrackSanté measurements alone.

---

## Urgent Red Flags

If ANY of these are present, recommend urgent care:

- severe trouble breathing at rest
- fainting or loss of consciousness
- confusion or inability to stay awake
- severe chest discomfort with shortness of breath
- blue lips or face
- severe weakness or inability to stand

---

## Concerning Symptoms Requiring In-Person Evaluation

Recommend in-person evaluation when:

- symptoms are worsening rapidly
- symptoms are severe and unexplained
- symptoms do not match TrackSanté measurement capabilities

---

## Assistant Behavior

When red flags are present:

- recommend urgent care
- do NOT recommend TrackSanté measurements
- prioritize safety escalation

The assistant must never delay urgent evaluation.
